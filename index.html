  • Identity and access

    Staff, service accounts and cloud roles with more access than they need.

  • Forgotten systems

    Old apps, test servers and subdomains your team no longer tracks.

Questions

Is Ogbon an insurance company?

No. Ogbon is a cybersecurity company. The cover in every plan is a warranty from Ogbon: if an attack gets through while you're protected, we pay for the loss, up to your plan's limit. Our promise is backed by a partner insurer licensed by NAICOM, and we manage your claim with you from start to finish.

Is the cover an insurance policy?

Not today. It's a warranty from Ogbon, backed by a partner insurer. We plan to offer a full cyber insurance policy, arranged through licensed partners, once we hold a broker or managing agent licence. Existing customers will be able to move across.

Will Ogbon slow down our laptops?

No. Ogbon connects to the security tool your laptops already run. If you don't have one, we'll install a light one that runs in the background.

What data can Ogbon see?

Settings, device health, login rules, and the code repositories you choose to connect. We don't read emails, chats or documents. Access is read-only and you can disconnect at any time.

How long does setup take?

About an hour of your IT team's time. Most companies are covered within a week.

What is ogbon-1?

ogbon-1 is Ogbon's own 30B security model. It reviews code, finds vulnerabilities and fixes them. Your engineers use it through Ogbon Harness or the Ogbon API, and it's included in every plan.

Can we keep our code on our own servers?

Yes. ogbon-1 runs on a single GPU, so you can host it yourself and your code never leaves your network. We never use customer code to train our models.

Do you cover money stolen through hacked accounts or leaked API keys?

Yes, up to your plan's limit. It's one of the most common ways businesses that move money lose it, so it's included in every plan.

What if we can't fix something in time?

Tell us before the due date and we'll agree a new date or another fix. Your cover is only affected if a critical issue is left open past its due date without telling us.

How quickly are claims paid?

We aim to decide within 15 working days of getting all documents, and to pay within 5 working days of approval.

Does Ogbon help with CBN and NDPA compliance?

Yes. Growth and Scale plans include audit evidence mapped to the CBN risk-based cybersecurity framework and the Nigeria Data Protection Act.
