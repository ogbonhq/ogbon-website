04 / Research

Every result, published.

We measure everything, publish every result, including the experiments that failed, and build on what works.

  1. E004 · In progress

    Hybrid serving of large MoE models

    Experts in system memory, everything else on one GPU. Cheaper per token than GPUs sized to fit them?

  2. E003 · Partial result

    Why AMX bf16 scales poorly across cores

    13.5 TFLOP/s at 8 threads, 5.0 at 16. Noise, kernel switching and power limits ruled out.

  3. E002 · Result

    CPU‑only language‑model serving

    7 to 20 times more per token than a loaded H100. Rejected, so we moved to hybrid serving.
