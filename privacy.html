Last updated 26 November 2026

1. Who we are

Ogbon Security Limited is a cybersecurity company registered in Nigeria. We decide how personal data is used on this website, for free security reports, and when we sell to and bill our customers.

When we work inside a customer's cloud accounts, code or other systems, the customer decides what we process and why. In that case the customer is the data controller and we process the data on its behalf, under our agreement with it.

Ogbon Security Limited, 14B Grace Anjous Street, Lekki Phase 1, Lekki, Lagos State. Email secure@ogbon.com or call 0710 714 4122.

2. What we collect

When you ask for a free security report:

  • Your company domain, work email, number of staff and industry.
  • Information about your domain that is already public, such as DNS and email settings, websites and services open to the internet, public code, and staff email addresses that appear in public data leaks.

When your company uses Ogbon:

  • Names, work emails and phone numbers of the people who use your account.
  • Billing details.
  • Configuration, access settings and login rules from the systems you connect, and the code repositories you choose to connect.
  • What you tell us about an incident or a claim.

When you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we keep what you send us.

When you visit ogbon.com, our server records your IP address, browser and the pages you ask for. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address.

We don't read emails, chats or documents in your systems.

3. How we use it, and our legal basis

  • To produce your free report and reply to you about it. Our basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in answering requests.
  • To provide monitoring, your fix list and incident response. Our basis is our contract with your company.
  • To provide your cover and handle claims, including giving Casava, the insurer that backs our warranty, what it needs to assess a claim. Our basis is our contract and our legal obligations.
  • To bill you and keep tax records. Our basis is our contract and our legal obligations.
  • To keep our service secure and prevent fraud. Our basis is our legitimate interests.
  • To meet legal and regulatory duties. Our basis is our legal obligations.

We only send marketing messages with your consent, and you can stop them at any time.

4. Your code and ogbon-1

We use the code you connect only to review it and to find and fix problems for you. We never use customer code to train our models. If you run ogbon-1 on your own servers, your code doesn't reach us.

5. Who we share it with

  • Casava, the insurer that backs our cover warranty, and its reinsurer, to handle claims.
  • Incident responders and investigators working on your incident.
  • Companies that host our systems, send our emails and messages, and process payments for us. Our contracts with them require them to protect the data and use it only for us.
  • Regulators such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC), the police or a court, when the law requires it or when you ask us to help you report an incident.

We don't sell personal data.

6. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of the companies we use may store data outside Nigeria. When that happens we use a safeguard the NDPA allows, such as a country with adequate protection, contract terms that protect the data, or your consent.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purposes above, including the time it takes to settle any claim and the records the law requires us to keep. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

8. How we protect it

We protect personal data with access controls, encryption and monitoring. Access to the tools you connect is read-only, and our staff can see customer data only when their work needs it.

If a breach of personal data is likely to put people at risk, we tell the NDPC within 72 hours of finding out, and we tell the people affected when the risk to them is high.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, or stop or limit how we use it.
  • Give you your data in a format you can take to another provider.
  • Stop using your data for marketing.

Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, email secure@ogbon.com. We may need to confirm who you are first. If you're not happy with our reply, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

10. Children

Ogbon is a service for organisations. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data in an important way, we'll email the contact on your account first.
