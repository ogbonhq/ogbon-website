01 / The problem

Physical AI needs faster, cheaper inference.

Robots, cameras and devices need to think in milliseconds.

Today’s inference was built for chatbots in distant data centres: slow round-trips, a high price per call, and systems that stall when the connection drops.

06 / About

From Africa, for the world.

Ogbon means wisdom in Yoruba.

We’re a team of researchers and engineers building in Lagos and London, founded by AB (founder of Mono, now part of Flutterwave) and Prakhar.

We believe the next frontier is moving from bits to atoms, and the hardest environments build the best technology.

07 / Join us

Help us build something that doesn’t exist yet.

We’re assembling a founding team of exceptional engineers and researchers to make large models run in real time in the physical world. We hire in Lagos, in London and remotely.

We’re looking for people who enjoy difficult problems, question conventional approaches, and want to push the boundaries of computing.

You don’t need a PhD or experience at a major technology company.

You need curiosity, strong technical ability, and the willingness to experiment.

Senior C++ Engineer

Remote/London/Lagos Full-time

Build high-performance software and optimise AI model execution on GPUs and edge hardware.

Responsibilities

  • Write and optimise the kernels and runtime that execute model operations on GPUs and edge devices.
  • Profile hot paths and remove bottlenecks in compute, memory access, and threading.
  • Use SIMD instruction sets such as AVX2, AVX-512, and NEON where they make a measurable difference.
  • Build benchmarks and tests that keep performance from regressing.
  • Work with research engineers to turn ideas into fast, correct code.

Requirements

  • Strong modern C++ (17 or later) and a solid understanding of what the compiler generates.
  • Experience with profiling tools such as perf or VTune.
  • A clear understanding of caches, memory layout, and multithreading on CPUs and GPUs.
  • Experience with numerical or high-performance code is a strong plus.
  • Comfort reading assembly when needed.
