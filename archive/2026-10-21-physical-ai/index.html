01 / The problem

The device is too small. The cloud is too far.

Physical AI has to sense, decide and act in milliseconds.

A robot arm, a drone or an inspection rig works inside a tight control loop. Miss it, and the machine hesitates, drops the part, or crashes.

The models good enough to act in the world don’t fit on the device. So teams shrink them until they fit, and lose the intelligence they wanted.

The public cloud is a region away: long, jittery round trips on GPUs tuned for chat, not for a loop that needs an answer every few milliseconds.

No one owns the full path from model to network to machine with real-time as the single goal.

02 / Why now

Physical AI is moving from demos to deployment

  • Models01 Foundation models for machines are here. Vision-language-action models let one model drive many tasks. They are large, and getting larger.
  • Fleets02 Fleets are coming. Humanoids, warehouse robots, drones and inspection rigs are entering production. Every unit needs inference, every second it runs.
  • Economics03 Inference is the recurring cost. Training is paid once. Inference is paid per machine, per hour. Whoever makes it fastest and cheapest owns the margin.
  • Networks04 Networks can now carry it. Private 5G, fibre to industrial sites and cheap local compute make a site or city inference cloud practical.

03 / How it works

Device. Site. City.

One stack, tuned across all three, decides what runs where.

The machine keeps its reflexes on the device. Heavier models run on a cluster close enough to stay inside the control loop.

Where each decision runs

Device

Reflexes and safe fallback

Site

Heavy models, on your floor

City

Shared capacity, close by

Our SDK runs on the machine and handles what must happen instantly. Heavier thinking streams to an Ogbon cluster on your site or in your city. If the link drops, the machine falls back safely on the device.

04 / What we build

We own the whole latency stack.

From the model to the network to the machine, every layer is built for one thing: real-time.

Bring your model and connect your machines. We handle the compute, and tune every layer together.

  1. 01

    Model compression

    Distillation, quantisation and pruning, tuned per model and per hardware target. Big-model behaviour at a fraction of the compute.

  2. 02

    Custom kernels

    Hand-written GPU kernels for the operators physical AI runs most, recovering the latency general-purpose runtimes leave behind.

  3. 03

    Real-time chunking

    Splits work between device and cloud and streams actions in chunks. The machine keeps moving while the next chunk computes.

  4. 04

    Specialised cloud

    Inference clusters at your site or in your city, tuned for control-loop latency, not chat throughput.

  5. 05

    SDKs

    Libraries for robots, drones and edge devices, with fallbacks, scheduling and telemetry built in. Drop it in, and real-time just works.

  6. 06

    Full solutions

    Where our latency and cost edge is largest, we run the whole stack for you: manufacturing lines, drone fleets, inspection and warehouses.
