01 / Our thesis

The world doesn’t have a computing shortage. It has a computing efficiency problem.

Billions of CPU cores already exist across the world.

Meanwhile, artificial intelligence increasingly depends on expensive, specialized GPUs.

We believe there’s another way.

What if we could unlock the computing power we already have, rather than always building more specialized hardware?

At Ogbon, we’re researching how software can transform ordinary CPUs into high-performance AI computing systems.

02 / Introducing VirtualGPU (VPU)

One virtual accelerator. Thousands of CPU cores.

VirtualGPU (VPU) is our proposed software-defined AI computing architecture.

It combines distributed CPU resources into a unified execution environment designed for demanding AI workloads.
