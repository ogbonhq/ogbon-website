What ogbon-1 does

  1. Reviews every change

    Connect GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket and ogbon-1 checks each pull request for security issues before it's merged.

  2. Finds vulnerabilities

    It scans your repositories for exposed secrets and API keys, injection flaws, broken login and access checks, and outdated dependencies with known issues.

  3. Fixes them

    Ask Ogbon Harness to fix an issue and ogbon-1 edits the files. Your engineers review every change before it goes in.

Part of your risk score

What ogbon-1 finds in your code goes on the same fix list as the rest of your security, with the naira it puts at risk. Your founders see the effect on the dashboard; your engineers see the details in Harness and in their pull requests.
