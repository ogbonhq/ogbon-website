Find and fix security gaps across your organisation

ogbon-1 is our 30B AI model built for security. It finds weak spots, spots attacks as they happen and prepares the fixes. It's included in every Ogbon plan: use it on your team's Mac and Windows laptops, across your code, cloud and servers.

Download for Mac Download for Windows

What ogbon-1 does

  1. Finds gaps everywhere

    It checks your apps, cloud accounts, servers, laptops and build pipelines for weak spots an attacker could use, like exposed keys, risky settings and accounts with too much access.

  2. Spots attacks as they happen

    It watches for hijacked accounts, unusual logins and suspicious activity on your systems and devices, and joins related alerts into one incident for your team.

  3. Fixes them

    ogbon-1 prepares the fix, from a cloud setting to a code change or a device policy. Your team approves it before it goes in.
