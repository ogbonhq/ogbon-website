If money has left your accounts

  1. Call your bank and ask them to freeze or recall the transfer.
  2. Contact us. We'll stay with you while you deal with the bank.
  3. Change passwords on the affected accounts and revoke any API keys that may be exposed.
  4. Keep every email, message and log. Don't delete anything.
  5. File a police report. We'll tell you what to include.

If your systems are locked or data has leaked

  1. Disconnect affected computers from the network, but leave them switched on.
  2. Contact us. Don't reply to the attackers or pay them.
  3. Our responders will join within four hours to contain the attack and find out what happened.
