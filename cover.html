What's not covered

These are the main exclusions. The full list is in your agreement.

  • Losses caused by a critical issue we flagged that wasn't fixed by its due date
  • Fraud carried out or approved by an owner or director
  • Attacks that started before your cover began
  • War, and outages of national power, telecoms or internet networks
  • Fines the law doesn't allow to be insured
  • Lost, stolen or damaged devices
  • Crypto assets

Staying covered

Your cover depends on six basics. Each one shows as green or red on your dashboard.

  1. Ogbon is connected to your websites, apps, APIs, cloud and code
  2. Two-step login is on for all admin and cloud accounts
  3. No critical issue is left open past its due date
  4. Payouts above your set limit need two approvals
  5. Backups are kept where attackers can't change them, and tested every quarter
  6. Incidents are reported to us within 72 hours

If something falls behind, we'll tell you and give you time to fix it before it affects your cover.

Cover is a warranty under your Ogbon agreement, not an insurance policy. You'll get the full warranty terms before you sign.
