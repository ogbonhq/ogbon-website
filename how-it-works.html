How Ogbon works

It starts with a free security report.
After about an hour of setup, Ogbon runs in the background.

  1. 1

    Get your free security report

    Tell us your company domain. We check what attackers can see from outside and show you your biggest risks and what they could cost, in naira. Our team then walks you through it and recommends a plan.

  2. 2

    Connect your systems

    Connect your cloud accounts on AWS, Google Cloud or Azure, your code, and the internal systems you want covered, with read-only access. Install the Ogbon app on staff laptops, on Mac or Windows. Setup takes about an hour.

  3. 3

    Fix what matters

    Ogbon lists the fixes that protect the most money. ogbon-1 prepares each fix, from a cloud setting to a code change, and your team approves it. Your score updates as you go.

  4. 4

    Get covered

    Once the basics are in place, including two-step login on admin accounts, payout approvals and backups, your cover starts the next day, backed by Casava.

  5. 5

    We keep watch

    Ogbon checks your systems every day and spots attacks as they happen. Report a problem on WhatsApp or from your dashboard at any time. A responder calls back within an hour, and we handle your claim.

Ogbon connects to AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Kubernetes, GitHub, GitLab and Bitbucket, and runs on Mac and Windows laptops. For systems that can't be reached from outside, ogbon-1 can run on your own servers.
